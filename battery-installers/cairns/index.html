Cairns — One of Australia’s Best Solar Locations

Cairns and Far North Queensland benefit from a tropical climate that makes solar generation excellent, particularly in the dry season from May to October. During these months, Cairns receives consistently clear skies and high solar radiation, making it one of Australia’s best locations for solar output. Even during the wet season, many homes generate meaningful solar production.

The tropical climate also means high air conditioning loads — cooling costs can be a significant portion of a household’s electricity bill in Cairns. Solar panels produce at their peak during the hottest, sunniest hours when air conditioning demand is also at its highest, making solar particularly cost-effective for Far North Queensland homes.

Battery storage adds a further advantage: surplus solar energy generated during the day can be stored and used to run cooling systems in the evening and overnight, reducing grid electricity costs throughout the day and night cycle.
