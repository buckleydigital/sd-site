Solar and Battery Storage in Hobart — What You Need to Know

Hobart is a city defined by its relationship with nature — Mount Wellington, the Derwent River, and one of Australia's most beautiful harbours. It's also a city with a unique energy story. Tasmania's electricity grid is predominantly powered by hydroelectricity, meaning the grid itself is already among Australia's cleanest. So why install solar and a battery in Hobart?

The answer is about energy costs and independence, not just carbon. Even with a hydro-dominated grid, Hobart households pay electricity bills — and those bills have risen over recent years. A home solar and battery system allows you to generate your own electricity during the day and store it for use in the evening, reducing how much electricity you purchase from the grid regardless of how that grid electricity is generated.

For Hobart homeowners who value energy independence, the ability to generate and store your own power provides security against future electricity price rises and gives you greater control over your household energy costs.

Is Solar Viable in Hobart's Climate?

Hobart receives lower sunshine hours than mainland Australian cities — this is a fact worth acknowledging honestly. The city's southern latitude and sometimes overcast winters mean solar panels will generate less electricity than equivalent systems in Brisbane or Darwin. However, lower solar generation does not mean solar is unviable in Hobart — it means system sizing matters more, and the financial case should be assessed carefully.

Spring and summer in Hobart can offer very good solar production, with long daylight hours and clear skies. Autumn is also a reasonable solar period. Winter is the most challenging time, with short days and cloud cover reducing generation. A well-sized system accounts for this seasonal variation.

Battery storage is particularly valuable in Hobart precisely because solar generation can be inconsistent. Every unit of solar electricity that gets stored and used at home rather than exported to the grid (often at a low feed-in tariff rate) adds to your financial return. For Hobart homes with electric heating, this is especially relevant — using stored solar for evening heating reduces your grid electricity draw during peak periods.

Cold Climate Heating Loads

Hobart's cold winters create significant heating demands for residential properties. Electric heating — whether reverse-cycle air conditioning, heat pumps, or resistive heaters — draws substantial power, particularly in the evenings and overnight when heating loads peak. A battery system charged during the day from solar can power some of this evening heating load from stored solar energy rather than grid electricity.

Battery System Costs in Hobart

Home battery systems in Hobart typically cost in the range of $8,000–$15,000 before rebates (figures approximate — verify current amounts at energy.gov.au). After the federal Cheaper Home Batteries Program (approximately 30% off), out-of-pocket costs may be approximately $5,500–$10,500 (figures approximate — verify current amounts at energy.gov.au).

Popular battery brands for Hobart installations include the BYD Battery-Box, known for its modular design that allows capacity to be scaled, and the Sungrow battery, which has a strong track record in varied Australian climates. Your CEC-accredited installer can advise on which system best suits your home's energy profile and Hobart's solar conditions.

Tasmania-Specific Programs

As of mid-2026, Tasmania does not have a state-level battery rebate equivalent to programs in Victoria or South Australia. However, energy programs can change — always check energytasmania.com.au or speak with your installer for the most current information. The federal Cheaper Home Batteries Program is available to eligible Tasmanian homeowners regardless of state-level programs.
