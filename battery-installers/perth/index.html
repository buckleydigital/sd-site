Perth's Exceptional Solar Resource

Perth and the south-west of Western Australia receive some of the highest solar radiation of any Australian capital city. The combination of Perth's Mediterranean-style climate — warm, dry summers and mild winters — and its latitude makes it one of the most favourable locations in the country for solar energy generation.

Perth homeowners with existing solar systems have in many cases already experienced strong bill reductions. Battery storage is the next step for many Perth households — enabling the capture of excess solar generation that would otherwise be exported to the Synergy grid at the relatively low Distributed Energy Buyback Scheme (DEBS) rate, and using it instead for evening household consumption.

Western Australia operates its own electricity grid (the South West Interconnected System, or SWIS) independently from the eastern states' NEM. This means the rules around solar exports, battery systems, and grid connection in Perth are set by the Economic Regulation Authority and Synergy, and your matched installer will be familiar with the relevant WA-specific requirements and processes.

Air conditioning loads are significant in Perth's hot summers, and many Perth households also have pools — both of which are excellent loads to pair with solar and battery systems. Your matched installer can advise on optimal system sizing to address your specific household energy profile.

Battery Rebates and Loan Schemes for Perth Homeowners

Perth homeowners have access to both a federal rebate and a state interest-free loan scheme for battery storage:

  • Federal Cheaper Home Batteries Program: Reduces the cost of eligible home battery systems by approximately 30% (figures approximate — verify current amounts at energy.gov.au). Applies nationally, including all Perth postcodes, for eligible systems installed by CEC-accredited installers.
  • Synergy Residential Battery Scheme — Interest-Free Loan: Synergy offers eligible customers an interest-free loan of up to $10,000 (figures approximate — verify current amounts at energy.gov.au) to help finance battery storage. This is a loan, not a grant — it must be repaid. Available to eligible Synergy customers only. Verify current terms, eligibility, and availability at synergy.net.au before making any financial decisions.
Important — Synergy scheme is a LOAN, not a grant: The Synergy Residential Battery Scheme provides an interest-free loan that must be repaid. It is not a rebate or grant. Always verify current scheme terms, eligibility criteria, and repayment conditions directly at synergy.net.au before proceeding.

For full current WA rebate and scheme details, visit our Western Australia Battery Rebates page. Always confirm current amounts and eligibility with a CEC-accredited installer or the relevant authority.
