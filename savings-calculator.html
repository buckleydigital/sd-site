How we calculate your savings

Our calculator uses real data for each state to give you the most accurate indicative estimate possible. Here's what we factor in:

☀️

Peak Sun Hours

Each state has different average daily peak sun hours ranging from 3.8 (Tasmania) to 5.5 (Northern Territory).

⚡

Electricity Rates

We use the average retail electricity rate for your state (ranging from 25c to 42c/kWh) to calculate bill savings.

💸

Feed-In Tariff

Excess solar exported to the grid earns a feed-in tariff. We use the typical state rate (5c–10c/kWh).

🏷️

STC Rebate

System costs shown are after the federal STC rebate has been applied - your installer deducts this upfront.
