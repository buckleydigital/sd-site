🔴 Western Australia Rebates 2026

Solar & Battery Rebates Western Australia 2026 — Complete Guide

Western Australia's exceptional solar resources, combined with the Synergy Residential Battery Scheme and the federal Cheaper Home Batteries Program, make 2026 one of the best years on record for eligible WA homeowners to add a home battery system. Understanding the difference between loans and grants is essential before making any financial decisions.

Federal Solar Rebate — How STCs Work in Western Australia

The Small-scale Technology Certificate (STC) scheme is Australia's primary federal mechanism for reducing the upfront cost of residential solar. When you install solar with a CEC-accredited installer in WA, your installer creates and surrenders STCs on your behalf, and the value is applied as a direct point-of-sale discount on your installation cost. No paperwork is required from you — the installer handles everything.

Western Australia is located in one of Australia's highest solar irradiance zones, which means WA solar systems generate more STCs than equivalent systems in cooler, less sunny regions. This translates to a larger point-of-sale STC discount for WA homeowners. For a typical 6.6kW solar system in the Perth metropolitan area, the STC discount can be approximately $2,500 to $5,000 (figures approximate — verify current amounts at energy.gov.au), though the exact amount depends on current STC market values and your specific location.

Always verify the current STC value at energy.gov.au and ensure your written quote from your installer clearly itemises the STC rebate as a separate line item.

Federal Battery Rebate — Cheaper Home Batteries Program

The federal Cheaper Home Batteries Program launched in 2025 and is funded through to 2030 with a $7.2 billion national budget. It is available to all eligible WA homeowners and provides approximately 30% off the installed cost of an eligible home battery system (figures approximate — verify current amounts at energy.gov.au).

For example, a battery system with a total installed cost of $10,000 may be available for approximately $7,000 after the federal rebate is applied (figures approximate — verify current amounts at energy.gov.au). The exact amount depends on the specific battery model, system configuration, installer pricing, and current program values. Your CEC-accredited installer will confirm the applicable discount and apply it on your behalf at the time of installation.

The federal battery rebate can be combined with the Synergy Residential Battery Scheme (described below) for eligible Synergy customers — making WA one of the strongest combined incentive environments in Australia for home battery installations in 2026.

Synergy Residential Battery Scheme — Important Details

LOAN, Not a Grant: The Synergy Residential Battery Scheme is an interest-free LOAN, not a grant. The full loan amount must be repaid to Synergy. This is a critical distinction. Verify current terms, availability, and repayment conditions at synergy.net.au before making any financial decisions.

Synergy's Residential Battery Scheme offers eligible Synergy electricity customers an interest-free loan of up to $10,000 (figures approximate — verify current amounts at energy.gov.au) to assist with the upfront cost of purchasing and installing a home battery system. The loan is interest-free, meaning you pay back only the principal amount you borrowed — there are no interest charges. However, the full principal must be repaid according to the agreed repayment schedule.

Key Details of the Synergy Scheme

  • Loan, not a grant: The money must be repaid. An interest-free loan reduces your upfront cost but does not permanently reduce the total amount you spend on the battery.
  • Synergy customers only: You must be a Synergy electricity customer to access this scheme. Customers of Horizon Power or other providers should check those providers' websites for relevant programs.
  • Eligibility criteria apply: Synergy sets specific eligibility requirements. Verify current criteria and availability at synergy.net.au.
  • Approved battery models: The loan may be restricted to approved battery models. Confirm with your installer that your chosen battery qualifies.
  • Program availability: The scheme's availability and terms can change. Always verify the current status before proceeding.

How Synergy Loan and Federal Rebate Can Stack

Eligible WA homeowners who are Synergy customers may be able to access both the Synergy Residential Battery Scheme interest-free loan (up to $10,000) (figures approximate — verify current amounts at energy.gov.au) and the federal Cheaper Home Batteries Program (approximately 30% off battery cost) (figures approximate — verify current amounts at energy.gov.au). Combined, these programs can significantly reduce your effective upfront cost and spread repayments — but the Synergy loan component must still be repaid in full.

Confirm stacking eligibility with your CEC-accredited installer and verify current terms at synergy.net.au and energy.gov.au.

What About Horizon Power Customers?

Homeowners in regional WA who are served by Horizon Power rather than Synergy should check the Horizon Power website for any relevant programs. All WA homeowners — whether Synergy or Horizon Power customers — can access the federal Cheaper Home Batteries Program, provided they use a CEC-accredited installer and select an eligible battery model.

Eligibility Criteria for WA Homeowners

To access the available programs in Western Australia, the following general requirements apply:

  • Property owner: You must own the residential property where the system is being installed.
  • CEC-accredited installer: You must use a Clean Energy Council (CEC) accredited installer for all federal programs. ReclaimPower only connects WA homeowners with CEC-accredited installers.
  • Synergy customer (for Synergy scheme): The Synergy Residential Battery Scheme is available to Synergy customers only.
  • Eligible battery model: Both the federal rebate and the Synergy scheme may require the battery to be on an approved list. Your installer will confirm which models qualify.
  • Residential property: Programs generally apply to residential dwellings, not commercial properties.

Always verify eligibility criteria with your installer, at synergy.net.au, and at energy.gov.au before making any financial decisions.

Is Solar and Battery Worth It in WA?

Western Australia is one of Australia's best states for solar generation. The Perth metropolitan area in particular benefits from extremely high annual sunshine hours and excellent solar irradiance, consistently placing WA among the top regions in the world for solar productivity. This means WA solar systems generate more energy — and therefore more savings — than equivalent systems in most other parts of Australia.

The combination of the federal Cheaper Home Batteries Program and the Synergy interest-free loan can significantly reduce the effective upfront cost of a home battery for eligible Synergy customers, making the payback period very attractive. A battery allows you to store surplus solar energy for evening use, maximising self-consumption and reducing reliance on grid power at peak tariff times.
