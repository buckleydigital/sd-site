Solar & Battery Rebates Australian Capital Territory 2026 — Complete Guide

The Australian Capital Territory offers some of the most comprehensive sustainable energy incentives in Australia. The ACT Sustainable Household Scheme — a zero-interest loan for a wide range of sustainable upgrades — can be combined with the federal Cheaper Home Batteries Program, giving eligible Canberra homeowners access to significant combined support for solar and battery installations.

Federal Solar Rebate — How STCs Work in the ACT

The Small-scale Technology Certificate (STC) scheme is Australia's primary federal mechanism for reducing the upfront cost of residential solar. When you install solar with a CEC-accredited installer in the ACT, your installer creates and surrenders STCs on your behalf. The value of those certificates is applied as a direct point-of-sale discount on your installation cost. You simply pay the reduced price — your installer handles all STC paperwork.

The ACT's location and climate result in a moderate STC quantity compared with sunnier northern states. Nevertheless, the STC rebate still represents a meaningful upfront discount for ACT homeowners. For a typical 6.6kW solar system installed in the Canberra region, the STC discount provides a useful contribution to system cost reduction (figures approximate — verify current amounts at energy.gov.au).

The STC value fluctuates based on market prices and changes regularly. Always verify the current STC discount with your installer and at energy.gov.au, and request a written quote that clearly itemises the STC rebate separately from other costs.

Federal Battery Rebate — Cheaper Home Batteries Program

The federal Cheaper Home Batteries Program launched in 2025 and is funded through to 2030. With a $7.2 billion national budget, it is the most significant home battery incentive ever offered at a federal level in Australia — and ACT homeowners are fully eligible.

The program provides approximately 30% off the installed cost of an eligible home battery system (figures approximate — verify current amounts at energy.gov.au). A battery system with a total installed cost of $10,000 may therefore cost approximately $7,000 after the federal rebate is applied (figures approximate — verify current amounts at energy.gov.au). Exact amounts depend on the battery model, system configuration, installer pricing, and current program values.

The federal battery rebate can be combined with the ACT Sustainable Household Scheme (described below) — making the ACT one of Australia's strongest combined incentive environments for home battery and solar installations in 2026. Your CEC-accredited installer applies the federal rebate on your behalf at the time of installation. Verify current program details at energy.gov.au.

ACT Sustainable Household Scheme — Important Details

LOAN, Not a Grant: The ACT Sustainable Household Scheme is a zero-interest LOAN, not a grant. It must be repaid. The zero-interest nature means you pay back only the principal with no interest charges — but the full amount borrowed must be repaid. Verify all current terms and eligibility at climatechoices.act.gov.au.

The ACT Sustainable Household Scheme is a flagship ACT Government program that offers eligible ACT households a zero-interest loan of up to $15,000 (figures approximate — verify current amounts at energy.gov.au) for a wide range of sustainable household upgrades. The scheme is designed to make the upfront cost of transitioning to cleaner energy more manageable by spreading payments over time — without any interest burden.

What the Scheme Covers

The ACT Sustainable Household Scheme covers a broad range of eligible sustainable upgrades, including:

  • Solar panel installations
  • Home battery storage systems
  • Electric vehicles and associated charging infrastructure
  • Other eligible energy efficiency improvements

The eligibility of specific products and upgrade categories is managed by the ACT Government and may be updated over time. Always check the current approved product list at climatechoices.act.gov.au before making any purchase decisions.

Key Details of the Scheme

  • Zero-interest loan, not a grant: The full amount borrowed must be repaid with no interest. The loan reduces your upfront payment burden but does not permanently reduce the total cost of your upgrade.
  • Maximum loan amount: Up to $15,000 (figures approximate — verify current amounts at energy.gov.au), subject to eligibility and program availability.
  • Broad range of eligible upgrades: Solar, battery, EV, and other sustainable household improvements may all be financed under a single loan.
  • ACT residents only: Applicants must reside in the ACT and the property must be an eligible ACT residential property.
  • Program availability: The scheme's terms and availability can change. Always verify at climatechoices.act.gov.au before proceeding.

How the Scheme and Federal Rebate Stack

Eligible ACT homeowners may be able to access both the ACT Sustainable Household Scheme zero-interest loan (up to $15,000) (figures approximate — verify current amounts at energy.gov.au) and the federal Cheaper Home Batteries Program (approximately 30% off battery cost) (figures approximate — verify current amounts at energy.gov.au) simultaneously. The federal rebate reduces the actual purchase price of the battery at the point of installation, while the Sustainable Household Scheme loan can cover the remaining portion of the cost (and potentially other upgrades like solar and EV charging).

Confirm stacking eligibility with your CEC-accredited installer and verify current terms at climatechoices.act.gov.au and energy.gov.au.

Eligibility Criteria for ACT Homeowners

To access the available programs in the ACT, the following general requirements apply:

  • ACT resident and property owner: You must reside in the ACT and own the residential property where the system is being installed.
  • CEC-accredited installer: You must use a Clean Energy Council (CEC) accredited installer for all federal programs. ReclaimPower only connects ACT homeowners with CEC-accredited installers.
  • Eligible property: The property must meet the program's residential property requirements. Verify specific criteria at climatechoices.act.gov.au.
  • Eligible products: Both the federal battery rebate and the Sustainable Household Scheme have approved product lists. Your installer will confirm which batteries and other products qualify.
  • Loan repayment capacity: For the Sustainable Household Scheme, as with any loan, you should ensure you can comfortably meet repayment obligations.

Always verify current eligibility criteria at climatechoices.act.gov.au and energy.gov.au before committing to any purchase.

Is Solar and Battery Worth It in the ACT?

The Australian Capital Territory experiences a temperate climate with good solar resource across most of the year, particularly during the long Canberra summers. While the ACT receives less solar irradiance than northern states, the combination of generous incentive programs and rising electricity prices still makes a solar-plus-battery investment financially compelling for eligible ACT homeowners.

The ACT Government has been one of Australia's most progressive territories in terms of clean energy policy, having achieved close to 100% renewable electricity procurement. This policy environment, combined with the Sustainable Household Scheme and federal battery rebate, creates an excellent incentive structure for homeowners wanting to reduce their energy bills and environmental footprint simultaneously.

A well-sized solar system significantly reduces daytime grid consumption, and a home battery extends those savings into the evening — allowing you to use your own solar energy rather than purchasing electricity from the grid at peak rates. With the zero-interest loan reducing the upfront barrier, 2026 is a strong time for eligible ACT homeowners to act.
